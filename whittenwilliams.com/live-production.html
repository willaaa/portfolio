Live Production

I have worked in live production for 8 years across 15+ stage productions working in construction, lighting and sound. I've worked with talented performers ranging from original and touring Broadway stars to 5th graders stepping on a stage for the first time. In a live production there are a million moving parts, and the world of technical production is mysterious and overwhelming to many performers. I remove this element of stress to allow the performers or directors to focus on what they do best. All of the performers I have worked with have had incredible stories and performances to share, and I takes great pride in being able to use my technical skills to help them share their work with their audiences. 
While recently my focus has largely shifted towards recorded studio productions, theatrical production is near and dear to my heart and I hope to make a triumphant return in the near future.
